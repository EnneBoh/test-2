How to play

  1. Take turns. Drop one sphere onto any peg that isn’t full. It falls and lands on top of the stack. Each peg holds four.
  2. Make four in a line. Rows, columns, upright stacks, flat diagonals and 3D diagonals through the cube — 76 lines in all.
  3. First to four wins. If all 64 spots fill up with no line, it’s a draw.

Players

  • Player 1 — pearl spheres, amber glow, round badge
  • Player 2 — obsidian spheres with a blue band, diamond badge

Controls

  • Mouse: hover a peg to preview, click to drop. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
  • Touch: tap a peg to preview, tap again to drop (change in Settings). One finger rotates, pinch zooms.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys choose a peg, Enter drops, Z undoes, R resets the view.
